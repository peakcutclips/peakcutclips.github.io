PeakCut Clips

Short clips from gaming livestreams

PeakCut Clips is a small, private tool used by one creator to turn long gaming livestreams into short vertical clips (YouTube Shorts) with subtitles, and to upload those clips to the creator's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Finds the most interesting moments in public livestream replays the creator is allowed to clip (paid clipping campaigns).
  • Makes a 9:16 vertical clip with subtitles.
  • After the channel owner approves each clip, uploads it to the owner's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), and reads that video's view count to track performance.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner who runs it. It is not a public service and does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.
