Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PeakCut Clips ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner to upload short clips to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Google user data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload access (scope youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own channel.
  • Basic channel information and video statistics (scope youtube.readonly): the channel name, and view counts of videos the app uploaded, to show how each clip performs.

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's approved clips and show their view counts to the owner. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and channel information are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on third-party servers. Data is protected by the security of that computer and is never transferred to other parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while a channel is connected. Disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
